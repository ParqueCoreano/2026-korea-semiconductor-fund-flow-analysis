# Project Plan

## 1. Project Background

The Korean semiconductor industry has been strongly influenced by
AI-related demand, particularly memory semiconductors and HBM.

This project investigates whether market momentum and capital
allocation have expanded beyond major memory semiconductor companies
toward semiconductor equipment, materials, and components.

The analysis focuses particularly on KOSDAQ-listed companies
within the semiconductor value chain.

## 2. Research Question

> Is Korea's semiconductor rally rotating beyond memory into
> semiconductor equipment, materials, and components,
> particularly KOSDAQ-listed companies, since July 2026?

## 3. Hypothesis

The initial hypothesis is that market momentum may have expanded
from major memory semiconductor companies toward companies involved
in semiconductor equipment, materials, and components.

However, price increases alone do not prove capital rotation.

Therefore, this project will examine multiple indicators:

* Relative stock performance
* Trading value and market activity
* Investor flows
* ETF-related flows and assets
* Market breadth
* Company fundamentals

## 4. Analysis Period

### Baseline Period

January 2026 – Present

### Core Analysis Period

July 2026 – Present

July 2026 is treated as a potential regime-change point
to be tested rather than a confirmed starting point of rotation.

## 5. Analytical Framework

The analysis will compare different segments of the
Korean semiconductor value chain.

### Memory

Major memory semiconductor companies and related market proxies.

### Equipment

Companies providing semiconductor manufacturing equipment.

### Materials

Companies providing semiconductor manufacturing materials.

### Components

Companies providing semiconductor-related components and parts.

### KOSDAQ Semiconductor Companies

KOSDAQ-listed companies within the semiconductor value chain.

## 6. Key Questions

### Q1. Performance Rotation

Did equipment, materials, and components outperform
major memory semiconductor companies after July 2026?

### Q2. Capital Flow

Is there evidence of increased capital allocation toward
equipment, materials, and component companies?

### Q3. Market Breadth

Is the potential rotation broad-based across multiple companies,
or driven by only a small number of stocks?

### Q4. Fundamentals

Do company fundamentals support the observed market movement?
